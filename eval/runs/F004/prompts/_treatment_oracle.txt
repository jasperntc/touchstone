
WHAT THE TEAM KNOWS ABOUT CERTIS

Three things about this provider that are not written down in the codebase.

1. A throttled call returns HTTP 200 with a body of
   {"status": "throttled", "results": []}. It does not raise and it is not an
   error response. That is the same shape as a holder Certis checked and found
   nothing adverse about, so an empty results list on its own tells you
   nothing; only "status" separates the two.

2. verify() is not idempotent for a holder whose review_state is
   "pending_review". Calling it again restarts that holder's review clock.

3. A null score means the holder was not checked. It is not a score of zero and
   it is not a failed check.
